# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Warehouse agents and administrators. Agents scan barcodes and assign products to warehouse zones. Admins manage users, products, and zones via a dashboard. Used on handheld devices and desktop browsers in warehouse environments.

## Product Purpose

A lightweight stock-management tool that tracks which products are in which warehouse zones. Primary job: scan a product barcode, pick a zone, confirm assignment. Secondary: admin CRUD for users, products, and zones with CSV bulk import/export.

## Brand Personality

Practical, efficient, trustworthy. Three words: clear, purposeful, reliable.

## Anti-references

- Cluttered legacy warehouse software with dense data tables and tiny controls.
- Generic SaaS dashboards that feel like spreadsheet skins.
- Overly decorative interfaces that slow down scanning workflows.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed first**: every screen must let an agent complete their task in under 10 seconds.
2. **Clarity over density**: one primary action per screen, obvious affordances, no competing calls-to-action.
3. **Feedback is trust**: every action (scan, assign, import) gives immediate, explicit confirmation.
4. **Mobile-native**: touch targets, readable at arm's length, works on warehouse handhelds.
5. **Restrained palette**: color carries meaning (status, action, alert), not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Touch targets minimum 44×44 px. Color is never the sole indicator of state. Reduced-motion respected.
